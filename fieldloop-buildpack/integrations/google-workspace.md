# FieldLoop v0.1 — Office Workflow (Google Workspace)

The receptionist's side of FieldLoop. Assumption: "Google Docs" = **Google
Workspace** (Gmail + Sheets). If bookings currently live in a free-form Google
Doc, v0.1 targets a **Google Sheet** (structured data needs a table) — either
migrated manually or generated by FieldLoop.

## 1. Decisions (locked)

| Question | Decision |
|----------|----------|
| Receptionist's console | **Office web app** (Next.js 15 + shadcn). PWA-installable for a dock icon. **No Electron.** |
| Existing CRM | **None.** FieldLoop's web app is the job-management system. |
| Office tooling to integrate | **Google Workspace** — Gmail (both directions) + Google Sheets (bookings/roster). |
| Slack role | **Alerts only** (dispatch/lead/OH&S) — already specced in `compliance-engine/webhooks.ts`. |
| Email depth | **Both directions** — inbound booking emails → job drafts; outbound confirmations + tech dispatch. |

## 2. Receptionist workflow

1. Booking arrives by phone or email. Email bookings go to
   `bookings@chatsworthconstructions.com.au`.
2. FieldLoop pulls unprocessed booking emails from the `FieldLoop/New` label,
   parses them, and creates a **job draft** (`status = 'draft_quote'`) linked to
   the matched division entity, then flags it for review.
3. The receptionist reviews/edits in the web scheduler and assigns a technician
   and time.
4. FieldLoop sends the **client confirmation email**, the **technician dispatch
   email**, and the **Slack dispatch alert**.
5. The **Roster/Status Sheet** auto-updates for office visibility.
6. On completion, the invoice syncs to Xero/MYOB (existing connectors).

## 3. Gmail (both directions)

### 3.1 Inbound — booking intake

- A `bookings@` mailbox receives booking emails.
- A Google Cloud **service account** with domain-wide delegation reads the
  `FieldLoop/New` label, parses messages, and re-labels them `FieldLoop/Processed`
  (idempotent — no double-creation).
- The parser extracts client name, address (AU street), phone (AU mobile), and
  notes; `job_type` defaults to `standard` and is set to `body_corporate` /
  `insurance_repair` by keyword (`strata`, `body corp`, `insurance`, `claim`).
- Parsed bookings create `jobs` rows; a Slack alert notifies the division channel
  that a new booking needs review.

### 3.2 Outbound — confirmations & dispatch

- Sent via Gmail API (service account, delegated to `bookings@`).
- Templates: client booking confirmation, technician dispatch notice, invoice
  follow-up. All carry the entity ABN/PIC in the footer.

## 4. Google Sheets (bookings & roster)

Two-way sync against the receptionist's spreadsheets:

| Sheet | Direction | Purpose |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| `Bookings` | Import → jobs | Her intake list (Client, Address, Phone, Job Type, Division, Notes) becomes job drafts |
| `Roster` | Jobs → export | Live read-only status (Job, Client, Address, Technician, Status) for her board |

## 5. Auth & scopes

- Google Cloud service account, domain-wide delegation to the `bookings@` mailbox.
- Gmail scopes: `gmail.modify`, `gmail.send`.
- Sheets scope: `spreadsheets` (share the target spreadsheets with the service
  account email as editor).
- Credentials from env (`GOOGLE_CLIENT_EMAIL`, `GOOGLE_PRIVATE_KEY`); never in
  the repo. See `integrations/gmail.ts` and `integrations/sheets.ts`.

## 6. Why not Electron / Slack / a CRM patch

- **Electron** adds an install/update surface for zero gain — the web app is the
  console and installs as a PWA.
- **Slack** is a notification channel, not a scheduling/data-entry surface.
- **CRM patch** — there is no CRM to patch; FieldLoop is the job system, and
  Google Workspace is the office's existing tooling we plug into.
