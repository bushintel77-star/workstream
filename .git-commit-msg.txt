fix(web): always clear canopy scan state, even on empty/no-drawing imagery

The AI canopy scan set `canopyScanning: false` only after `proposeFromCanopyImage`
and `mutate` completed successfully. If the image had no drawing or the colour
heuristic failed, those calls could throw and the "AI scanning aerial for
canopy…" pill would spin forever.

Wrapped the whole `ingestCanopyImage` body in `try/finally` so the scan flag
and `aiBusy` always reset, regardless of whether any proposals were produced.
